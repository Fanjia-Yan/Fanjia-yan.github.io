Fanjia Yan

I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon AGI, where I work on agentic post-training for language models. I am a core contributor to Nova 2 Lite, working on agentic reinforcement learning, tool-use training data, and agent evaluations.

I received my M.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science and my undergraduate degrees in Computer Science and Applied Mathematics from UC Berkeley, where I worked on Language Model Agents and Tool usage. I have previously interned at Cohere, Nvidia, Amazon, and Aqueduct.
